# Sleight School Practice Tracker

Track your progress across the syllabus. Check off items as you hit the milestone criteria to graduate from foundations to routines and flourishes.

## Phase 1: Foundation Building (Weeks 1–3)
* [ ] **Key Card & Peeks:** Execute a bottom peek and key card tracking 10 times in casual conversation without looking down at your hands.
* [ ] **Overhand Shuffles:** Perform 20 clean overhand shuffles keeping the top and bottom stock exactly where intended.
* [ ] **Hindu Shuffle Force:** Practice the force until the stop-action and display flow without a pause or hesitation.
* [ ] **Self-Workers (21-Card / Clock Trick):** Run through these smoothly while maintaining natural patter.

## Phase 2: Sleight Integration (Weeks 4–7)
* [ ] **Double Lift:** Practice the turnover in front of a mirror until 20 consecutive lifts look identical to a single card.
* [ ] **Elmsley & Jordan Counts:** Count 4 cards back and forth to a metronome-even rhythm without pausing or flashing hidden faces.
* [ ] **Classic / Riffle Forces:** Successfully execute 10 forces on different people, ensuring the spectator believes their choice was completely free.
* [ ] **Top Change & Glide:** Master the clean substitution and second-from-bottom deal under conversational cover.

## Phase 3: Routine Construction (Weeks 8–11)
* [ ] **Ambitious Card:** Build a 3-phase routine combining a top control, a double lift, and a clean insertion.
* [ ] **Triumph:** Master the mix-and-reveal sequence so the cleanup hits hard after a seemingly randomized shuffle.
* [ ] **Biddle Trick:** Execute the mid-count steal cleanly while keeping the spectator engaged in conversation.
* [ ] **Oil and Water / Twisting the Aces:** Integrate count-based routines with natural storytelling and rhythm.

## Phase 4: Flourish & Polish (Weeks 12+)
* [ ] **Cuts & Fans:** Perform a fluid *Charlier Cut*, *Thumb Fan*, and *Dribble* on demand.
* [ ] **The Pass:** Drill the invisible cut daily until the packet shift registers zero visual disturbance.
* [ ] **Advanced Flourishes (Spring / Sybil / One-Handed Fan):** Master high-tension releases and multi-packet false cuts.
* [ ] **Set Performance:** Link any three routines from Part 3 into an uninterrupted 10-minute performance set.
