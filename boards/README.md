# Step boards

240 step-board PNGs covering the full catalog, no gaps: the original twelve (t26, t41, t50, t51, t61, t62, t71, t74, t81, t90, t91, t97) plus t101-t240, and the remaining 88 tricks of t1-t100, added 2026-10-21 by the same method.

Each board is one trick: one panel per step of its animation scene, with the step label and the catalog learner caption typeset under the photo. Captions are verbatim from the animation catalog (main, db6c5d88).

Method: textless photorealistic panel photos generated per step, captioned by typesetting (never in-image text). Boards were audited panel by panel against the catalog on 2026-10-21; the FAIL and MAJOR defects found were regenerated and re-verified before this upload.

Not wired into the app - reference material only.
